# Work With Me Closing Script

## Phase 1 — Open
Tell me a bit about your situation. What feels unclear or stuck right now?

Listen fully. Do not interrupt too quickly.

## Phase 2 — Clarify
What have you already tried so far?

Where do you feel things are not working?

Find the real friction, not just the surface description.

## Phase 3 — Reframe
From what you’re describing, it’s not just a lack of motivation. It’s a lack of structure.

Use this as the diagnosis point. Calm, direct, and clear.

## Phase 4 — Position the Offer
What I do is help you turn this into a clear structure and execution path, so you don’t stay stuck in thinking.

Keep this simple and direct.

## Phase 5 — Match the Offer
If they are early-stage:

I’d suggest starting with a Strategic Session to clarify everything and define a short execution path.

If they are more serious and committed:

I think the three-month structure would be more effective for your situation.

## Phase 6 — Handle Price
If they say it feels expensive:

That makes sense. The real question is whether staying in this situation has a cost as well.

Reframe without pressure.

## Phase 7 — Close
Would you like to move forward with this?

Simple. No over-explaining.

## Avoid
- over-explaining
- justifying price too much
- sounding needy
